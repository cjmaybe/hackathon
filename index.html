Official Challenge Rules

✦
  1. 01.The registration fee is ₹300 per participant.
  2. 02.Each team must consist of a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 4 members.
  3. 03.All code and game development work must be completed within the 24-hour hackathon period.
  4. 04.All participants must arrive between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM on 17 September 2026. No participant will be permitted to enter after 10:30 AM.
  5. 05.The project will be evaluated through three review stages: First Review: 50% project completion Second Review: 75% project completion Third Review: 100% project completion
  6. 06.Decision of the judges will be final and binding.
